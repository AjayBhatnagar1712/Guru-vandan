Privacy Policy

Guru Vandan is built as a calm devotional companion for satsang, meditation, aarti, and Sadguru wisdom. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, Guru Vandan may collect your name, email address, Firebase user ID, language preference, and devotional routine activity such as meditation completion, satsang completion, streak counts, and quote interactions.

The current Google Play build uses Google sign-in. Phone sign-in is not enabled in this build and the app does not ask users for a phone number.

How We Use Information

Account Functionality

To keep your profile, name, language choice, and signed-in app experience working across app sessions.

Routine Progress

To show meditation continuity, routine history, satsang completion, and personal progress inside the app.

Wisdom Content

To display Sadguru wisdom quotes in the language selected by the user.

App Maintenance

To help the admin understand whether app content and routine features are working properly.

Sharing And Sale Of Data

Guru Vandan does not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertisers. Data is processed through Firebase services used for authentication, database storage, and app functionality.

Storage And Security

User data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database. Data is transmitted using secure connections where supported by Firebase and Google services.

No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards and only collect data needed for the app's devotional routine features.

Your Choices

You may sign out of the app at any time. Signing out does not delete your account data. To request account and associated data deletion, use the deletion page below.

Children
