About Gurudev

Maharshi Mehi Paramhans

Maharshi Mehi Paramhans was one of the most respected saints and spiritual masters of the Sant Mat tradition in India. Born in Bihar, he dedicated his life to spreading the message of inner meditation, self-realization, universal love and peace. He emphasized the practice of Surat Shabd Yoga and taught that true spirituality lies beyond caste, religion and social divisions.

Through his profound writings, discourses and compassionate guidance, he inspired millions of devotees to walk the path of devotion, simplicity, morality and spiritual awakening. His teachings continue to guide seekers toward inner harmony and realization of the Divine within every soul.
